# 🚗 RoadPilot AI

An AI-powered Android driving assistant built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

## Vision

RoadPilot AI aims to transform an Android phone into a smart in-car assistant that provides:

- Voice-controlled navigation
- Dashcam recording
- AI assistance
- Music control
- Vehicle information
- Emergency assistance
- Offline and online functionality

---

# Technology Stack

- Kotlin
- Jetpack Compose
- MVVM Architecture
- StateFlow
- Navigation Compose
- CameraX
- Google Maps SDK
- SpeechRecognizer
- TextToSpeech
- Room Database
- Hilt
- Retrofit
- Gemini AI (later)
